How the score is estimated

The complete in-game score formula has not been published. The optimizer uses the pieces that are in the game's master data and fills the gaps with the assumptions listed below. Absolute numbers are estimates; the comparison between units is what the optimizer is built for.

1. Member stats

Each card's Performance, Technique and Sense come from its level curve and stat split, plus the Bloom (potential) bonus (+10% for ★4/★5, +15% for ★3 at full bloom). Flat bonuses from holomem board tiles are added, then all percentage bonuses: passive skills, the leader outfit skill, leader-type board tiles and memories (+0.2% per memory from the Memory Stand table).

2. Unit Score

Unit Score = Σ (P + T + S) of the five members × (1 + Member Upgrade Bonus). The Member Upgrade Bonus is the sum of every owned card's level bonus (Lv 20+), capped at 50%, exactly as the in-game help describes.

3. Notes

Each note scores Unit Score × song coefficient × note coefficient × (1 + combo bonus). Note coefficients come from the LiveNote table (PERFECT: tap/hold 1.00, flick 1.05, hold ticks 0.10; AUTO: 0.80 and no combo bonus). The combo bonus is +1% per 100 combo up to +10%. Chart timings, special-skill markers and the combo at each marker come from parsed charts; songs without chart data use evenly spaced notes.

4. Skills

  • Active skills are checked every cooldown (from the start of the song) with their activation chance and last for their duration. When several are active, only the highest effect counts (in-game help). The optimizer uses the expected value of that maximum.
  • Special skills fire once, at the chart's special markers in formation order (slot 1 first). Score Support multiplies active Score UP while it lasts; Skill Rate UP raises the activation chance of checks made during it.
  • Passive skills and leader outfits apply when their conditions hold (type count, unit members, leader, song singer). When a skill targets "2 Pure Type Members", the members with the highest base total are assumed to receive it.
  • Skill levels follow Bloom: 1 → Active Lv 2, 3 → Special Lv 2, 4 → Passive Lv 2.
  • Judgement Boost and LIFE recovery do not add score under ALL PERFECT play and are ignored. LIFE conditions follow the "LIFE stays full" option.

5. Holomem board

Holomem Rank gives board points (e.g. 191 at rank 33, 361 at rank 50). Each holomem's real board (153 tiles, laid out in one of four shapes) is simulated tile by tile: a tile can be unlocked when it touches an unlocked tile, you have the points and the Dream Rank it needs. Set up each board in My Data → Holomems exactly like in the game, or use Auto (Leader / Member / Support focus, like 自動設置) which spends the points along the cheapest paths. Holomems you have not edited are auto-set with your chosen focus. Note that auto setup assumes you have the unlock materials; if the game shows fewer unlocked tiles, edit the board to match.

  • Leader tiles (red) apply to the whole unit when that holomem is the leader; some only on songs they sing.
  • Member tiles (blue) boost that holomem's card when it is in the unit (flat stats, stat %, active skill rate and frequency).
  • Support tiles (green) apply to every unit, even when that holomem is not in it.
  • Song tiles (yellow) add a score bonus on songs by that holomem (max +10% total).
  • Connect effects from cards placed on Connect tiles are not modelled yet.

6. Search

Leaders are screened with a baseline unit, then each promising leader gets a greedy fill and a swap-until-no-improvement search over your cards (one card per holomem). The best units are then tried in all 120 formation orders.

Data

Master data: HolodoriDB. Chart timelines: parsed holodori charts.
